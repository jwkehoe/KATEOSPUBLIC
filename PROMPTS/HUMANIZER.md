# Humanizer
Use when asked to make a draft sound natural or apply the Humanizer module.
Preserve the author's meaning, voice, claims, citations, and technical detail.
Use concrete words, active verbs, and connected prose.
Remove filler, formulaic transitions, needless repetition, and excessive hedging.
Vary sentence length where it improves the flow. Keep useful structure.
Do not invent experience, evidence, or certainty to make the prose sound personal.
Return the revised text; flag any ambiguity that requires the author's judgment.
